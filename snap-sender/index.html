Black Snap Sender

Generates a black 9:16 image and hands it to Snapchat.

Download image

On mobile this opens the share sheet — pick Snapchat, then choose your friend and hit send inside the app. Picking the friend has to happen inside Snapchat; there is no API for a website to list your friends or send on your behalf.

Desktop: the button above shares a link to this page via Snap's Creative Kit. To send the actual black snap, use this page on your phone.
